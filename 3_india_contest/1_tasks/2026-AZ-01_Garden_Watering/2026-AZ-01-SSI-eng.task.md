## Body

A Beaver wants to water all the trees in the garden. He only moves on the pathways. 

The lengths of the pathways are: 

teak – deodar: 30 meters 

deodar – oak: 50 meters 

sal - oak: 60 meters 

deodar – sal: 20 meters 

teak – sal: 50 meters 

## Question/Challenge - for the brochures

In what order should Beaver water the trees so that his route was as short as possible? 

## Question/Challenge - for the online challenge

In what order should Beaver water the trees so that his route was as short as possible?
