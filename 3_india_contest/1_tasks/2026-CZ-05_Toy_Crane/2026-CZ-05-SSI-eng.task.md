## Body

The music player has two buttons marked with the numbers 1 and 0.

When 1 is pressed, the player turns on and music starts playing. If the player is turned on and 1 is pressed again, the music continues to play without interruption. The player does not turn on again. 

When 0 is pressed, the player turns off and the music stops playing.

At the beginning of the day, the player was turned off. At the end of the day, this sequence was in its memory:

101100101110010

## Question/Challenge - for the brochures

How many times has the player been turned on during the day?
