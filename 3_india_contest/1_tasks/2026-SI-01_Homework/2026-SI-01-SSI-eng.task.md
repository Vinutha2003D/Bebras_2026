## Body

Sara does her homework following these rules: 
- she does one assignment at a time; 
- at each step, she chooses the unfinished assignment that takes the shortest time; 
- after completing an assignment, she takes a break for 5 minutes. 

The table below shows all the assignments she has to complete and the time needed to complete each assignment

Assignment table 
:--------- | :----------
Assignment | Time needed
Maths      | 30 min
English    | 20 min
Science    | 45 min
Music      | 25 min
Computing  | 60 min


50 minutes after starting the first assignment, Sara added a 40-minute dog walk to the list of assignments.


## Question/Challenge - for the brochures

In which order will Sara complete all of her assignments?

D) English, Music, walking the dog, Maths, Science, Computing 

## Answer Explanation
The correct answer is D)

The correct answer is English, Music, Math, walking the dog, Science, Computing.

Sara started with the assignment which requires the shortest amount of time to complete it,
which is English. She took 20 minutes to finish it.

Then she did the second shortest assignment, that is music, which took her 25 minutes.

For her third assignment, Sara answered the maths assignment, which took her 30 minutes.

While doing maths, she decided she had to go for a 40-minute walk with her dog. However,
she first finished her maths assignment.

Since the time for walking the dog is less than the time required for the remaining
assignments (Science and Computing), Sara took her dog out for a walk as the fourth
assignment.

Next Sara answered her science assignment, which took her 45 minutes.

Finally Sara answered her computing assignment, which took her 60 minutes.

The other options are incorrect for the following reasons.

Maths, English, Science, Music, Computing, walking the dog is an incorrect option as the first homework assignment to be completed Maths is not the one that requires the shortest amount of time to complete it.

Computing, Science, walking the dog, Maths, Music, English is an incorrect option as the first
homework assignment to be completed Maths is not the one that requires the shortest amount of time to complete it.

English, Music, walking the dog, Maths, Science, Computing is an incorrect option because the order should be English, Music, Math, and then walking the dog. So incorrect order.

English, Maths, Music, walking the dog, Science, Computing is an incorrect option as the order should be English, Music, Math, and then walking the dog. So incorrect order.

## This is Informatics

Sara decided which assignment to do first by using the shortest job first rule: she started with
the assignment that would take the least time to complete among the unfinished assignments
and finish it before starting another.

Shortest Job First is a scheduling rule computers use to decide which task the CPU should
do next. The CPU chooses the task with the expected shortest execution time from the list of
not yet completed tasks. The tasks may be added at different times, but the new task is only
chosen when the current task is completed.

Between tasks, the computer needs a very short time to save the current data and prepare
the data needed for the next task.

## This is Computational Thinking
To solve this task you would use: algorithmic thinking and decomposition.

To solve this task, you must understand and follow all the rules that define the scheduling
algorithm. Besides, you must order the assignments in increasing order of the time needed
and simulate the execution of the algorithm. This involves both algorithmic thinking and
decomposition.
