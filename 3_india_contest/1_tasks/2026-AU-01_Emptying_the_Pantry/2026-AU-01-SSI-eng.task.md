## Body

Bhanu invited his friends to the party. He wants to bake a cake. 

At home he has: flour, sugar, eggs, butter, cream, fruit 

Bhanu only knows how to bake four cakes. For each of them, he has a list of the ingredients. 

Sponge cake: flour, sugar, eggs, butter, milk. 

Swiss roll: flour, sugar, eggs, butter, cocoa. 

Fruit fizz: flour, sugar, eggs, cream, fruit. 

Cheesecake: flour, sugar, butter, cottage cheese, fruit.
