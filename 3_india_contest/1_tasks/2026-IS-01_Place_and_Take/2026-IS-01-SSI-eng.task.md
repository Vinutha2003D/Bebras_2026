## Body

A robot is stacking boxes of fruits in the warehouse.

The actions are as follows:

Place the fruit box on top of the stack.

Take the box from the top of the stack and put it on the cart.

The robot starts a stack and perform the following actions:
1. Place the apples
2. Place the bananas
3. Place the oranges
4. Take
5. Take
6. Place the pears
7. Place the lemons
8. Place the grapes
9. Take
10. Take
